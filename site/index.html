Methodology

Storage adequacy places today's fill level within the lowest and highest levels seen in the same ±14-day calendar window over the previous five years: 0 = at the 5-year low, 100 = at the 5-year high. It is a position in a range, not a statistical percentile.

Net imports are entries minus exits at every point where gas crosses the EU-27 border, plus LNG terminal send-out, as reported by the EU-side operator on the ENTSOG Transparency Platform. Intra-EU flows are excluded, and transit through non-EU countries (e.g. TurkStream via Serbia) nets out within its corridor. Pre-2023 Russian volumes are understated: points that ENTSOG has since retired (Yamal via Poland) are missing from its current metadata.

Supply security is 100 × (1 − HHI/10,000), where HHI is the Herfindahl-Hirschman index of corridor shares in that day's positive net imports. It measures concentration, not reliability, and treats all LNG as one corridor even though LNG itself comes from many countries.
